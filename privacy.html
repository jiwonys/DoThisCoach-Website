Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 27, 2026

Overview

DoThis helps recreational athletes coordinate sport, strength training, nutrition, recovery, and changing schedules. This policy explains the information the app may collect, how it is used, and how to contact us.

Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the app, DoThis may collect account information, onboarding answers, goals, workout and sport schedules, workout and set logs, exercise preferences, food logs, weigh-ins, energy and recovery check-ins, soreness or pain feedback, barcode or food search activity, coach messages, subscription status, app diagnostics, bug reports, and support messages. This information is provided directly by you, generated through your use of app features, or received from Apple and RevenueCat for account and subscription access.

How We Use Information

We use information to provide daily goals, workout generation, nutrition targets, food logging, coaching, progress history, and coordinated plan recommendations. For example, DoThis may use a sport session, missed workout, schedule change, energy check-in, or soreness report to recommend a change to training, nutrition, or recovery. We may store the evidence, confidence, explanation, approval, or undo state associated with a material plan adjustment so the app can stay consistent across sessions. We also use information for subscription access, support, safety improvements, product analytics, and bug fixes.

AI Features

Some app features use OpenAI to generate or summarize fitness and nutrition guidance. When you consent to use these features, DoThis may send the text you enter and relevant profile, goal, schedule, workout, food, weight, energy, recovery, preference, or coaching context needed to answer your request. DoThis does not send every piece of account history with every request. Avoid entering sensitive medical or personal information that you do not want processed for app functionality.

Subscriptions

Purchases and subscription management are handled by Apple and RevenueCat. DoThis receives subscription status information needed to unlock premium features, but does not store your full payment card details.

Data Retention and Deletion

We keep information for as long as needed to provide the app, maintain records, resolve issues, improve safety, and comply with legal obligations. You can request account deletion or support by emailing help@dothiscoach.com.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent to help@dothiscoach.com.
