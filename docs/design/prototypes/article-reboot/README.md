# Article-identity homepage prototypes

These are working hero-plus-selector prototypes for the requested homepage reset. Production homepage files remain unchanged until a direction is approved.

## Grounded direction set

1. Arena Aperture — sport selection opens a ruled portrait window.
2. Game-Tape Contact Sheet — stills and app evidence behave like selected film frames.
3. Athlete Signal Split — article-night texture, a measured signal grid, command copy, and a synchronized athlete plane.
4. Court-Line Technical Sheet — court markings organize copy, state, and product proof.
5. Locker Credential — one athlete identity and one next-session credential.
6. Training Issue Cover — an editorial sports-cover composition with restrained product proof.
7. Tunnel Threshold — the visitor moves from preparation darkness toward the selected sport.

Impeccable concept seed `fc520847` assigned grounded candidate 3. Candidate 1 remains the strongest familiar alternate.

## Challenger evaluation

- Miura fold: declined on athlete identification and product clarity. Kept discipline: one selection must update the entire composition.
- Coptic binding: declined on athlete identification and product clarity. Kept discipline: state relationships remain visible instead of hidden inside animation.
- Broadcast teletext: competitive on athlete/sports recognition, weaker on modern product clarity. Kept discipline: exact grid, direct state controls, and restrained signal motion without retro costume.
- Shader portal: declined on product clarity and performance fit. Kept discipline: one authored atmospheric transition rather than scattered effects.
- Patent sheet: declined on athlete identification and emotional fit. Kept discipline: explicit mapping between selection, evidence, and destination.
- Fight poster: competitive on athlete identification, weaker on product clarity. Kept discipline: collision-scale typography and uncompromising hierarchy without boxing imagery or newsprint styling.

## Approval bar for full implementation

- Article palette and typography feel continuous with `/articles/`.
- First CTA and all six sports are visible at `390 × 844`.
- No headline exceeds `48px` on mobile.
- No hidden selector overflow, horizontal page overflow, fake metrics, or guaranteed performance claim.
- Selection updates media, copy, selected state, URL, and exact `/app/.../` route atomically.
- Full implementation later uses one readable real product screen, not a three-screen pile.
- Final homepage must score at least `90/100` under the independent beauty scorecard.
