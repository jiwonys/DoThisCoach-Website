# DoThis marketing product truth

Verified 2026-08-29 from the shipping App Store page, current website source, application asset manifests, and real simulator/App Store composites.

## Verified

- Product title: DoThis: Train For Your Game.
- App Store ID: `6771322181`.
- Category and supporting label shown by Apple: Health & Fitness; Workouts, Nutrition & Recovery.
- Supported acquisition states: General, Soccer, Basketball, Pickleball, Tennis, and Volleyball.
- Current product evidence includes Athlete Week, sport-aware workouts, Coach context, nutrition/macro logging, progress, and workout import.
- The six `/app` routes use a fixed allowlist of App Store custom product-page identifiers.
- The current website is static HTML/CSS/JavaScript deployed through GitHub Pages.

## Claims allowed for Phase 1 prototypes

- “Train for your game. Not just the gym.”
- “Workouts built around your sport, with nutrition and progress tracking in the same app.”
- Sport-specific benefits taken from approved App Store metadata and boards.
- “View on the App Store” or “Get DoThis” as outbound actions.
- The one-time 7-day Premium Preview begins after the first personalized workout is ready, requires no payment, and does not charge automatically.
- After Preview, local workouts, logs, and progress remain available; new personalized AI features and cloud sync require Premium.

## Not yet approved for publication

- Testimonials or customer endorsements.
- Conversion, install, or performance-lift claims.
- Awards, virality, injury prevention, or guaranteed sport outcomes.
- Premium pricing or renewal terms until reconciled against current App Store in-app purchases.
- Current App Store rating as marketing proof without owner approval.

## Evidence boundary

App Store marketing boards contain approved campaign composition and real simulator UI. They may be cropped or resized but must not be placed inside another phone frame. Raw simulator captures are real app UI using fictional staging personas. Generated athlete imagery is campaign art, not customer evidence.
