# Phase 1 art directions

Both directions are isolated prototypes. Neither replaces the production homepage.

## Direction A: Field Manual

Light editorial composition using warm paper, oversized black/green type, ruled evidence, and two approved sport boards shown without extra phone frames.

Strengths:

- Fastest first-time comprehension.
- Strongest contrast between live HTML copy and dark product evidence.
- Selector, benefit, imagery, and App Store destination read as one decision.
- Broadly accessible for recreational athletes without looking like a bodybuilding site.

Risk:

- The warm editorial palette is related to the current DoThis site and must continue evolving through the full page to avoid feeling like a reskin.

## Direction B: Night Match

Dark athletic composition using arena black, sport-specific signal color, hard structural rails, and two full-height approved boards.

Strengths:

- Strongest immediate sport energy.
- App Store boards feel native to the surface because their dark environments continue into the layout.
- Sport changes feel more dramatic while keeping the interaction purposeful.

Risk:

- Dark athletic sites easily collapse into the generic black-and-neon gym category. Real sport evidence prevents that here, but the full homepage would need disciplined pacing and light relief.

## Recommendation

Proceed with Field Manual. It communicates the product and App Store action more clearly while still letting the dark approved boards carry athletic intensity. Night Match remains the stronger campaign-style alternative when visual punch outranks broad product comprehension.

No elements should be blended across directions until the owner chooses one.
