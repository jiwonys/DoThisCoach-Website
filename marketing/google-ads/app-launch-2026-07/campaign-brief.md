# DoThis App Campaign Brief
## Campaign Position

DoThis turns fitness and nutrition goals into the next useful action: a workout, a daily plan, a faster food log, or an adjustment based on progress.

Primary promise: less planning, more consistent action.

The campaign deliberately avoids cheapest, guaranteed, medical, and transformation claims. Creative should feel attainable, capable, and calm rather than punishing or appearance-obsessed.

## Google Ads Text Assets

Each asset is written to work independently because Google may combine them in any order.

1. `Your Plan, Ready Every Day` (26)

### Descriptions (90 characters maximum)

3. `Generate workouts, track meals, and adapt your plan as training and recovery change.` (84)

Replace the existing `Cheapest Personal Strength...` description. Cheapest is an unverified comparative claim, and the sentence undersells the product as a price play.

## Creative Concepts

### Adaptive Training

- Audience signal: gym users, strength training, workout planners, fitness apps
- Message pairing: `Train Smarter With DoThis`
- Product proof to emphasize after install: goals, equipment, constraints, training history

### Effortless Food Tracking

- Audience signal: calorie tracking, meal logging, nutrition apps, weight management
- Message pairing: `Log Food Without the Fuss`
- Product proof to emphasize after install: photo estimate, typed estimate, barcode, recent foods

### Busy Professional

- Audience signal: home workouts, short workouts, productivity, working professionals
- Message pairing: `Fitness Built Around You`
- Product proof to emphasize after install: schedule-aware plans and shorter sessions

### Volleyball Performance

- Audience signal: volleyball, strength and conditioning, athletic performance
- Message pairing: `Your Plan, Ready Every Day`
- Product proof to emphasize after install: sport context, preparation, recovery, constraints

## Image Inventory

All files are text-free JPGs under `final/`, use safe central composition, and are below Google's 5 MB limit.

- 4 landscape images at 1200 x 628 (1.91:1)
- 4 square images at 1200 x 1200 (1:1)
- 4 portrait images at 1200 x 1500 (4:5)

Google may crop or modify assets. Keep all 12 variants instead of relying on automatic cropping.

## Creative Test Matrix

Run all four concepts in the same initial asset group so Google can learn. Do not change copy or images during the first learning window unless an asset is disapproved or clearly broken.

After enough conversion volume:

1. Keep assets rated Best or Good.
2. Replace Low assets one concept at a time.
3. Do not judge on clicks alone; prioritize cost per meaningful activation and subscription.
4. Test a second asset group with real app screens only after the lifestyle baseline has data.

## Conversion Measurement Checklist

Before optimizing beyond installs, confirm these events are actually connected to Google Ads through the app's analytics stack:

1. `first_open`
2. onboarding completed
3. daily plan generated
4. workout generated
5. first food logged
6. trial or subscription started
7. purchase / subscription renewal

Recommended optimization order:

1. Start with installs only when downstream event volume is too low.
2. Move to onboarding completed or first useful action when event volume is reliable.
3. Optimize for trial/subscription only after attribution and event volume are trustworthy.

Do not optimize directly for purchase while purchase volume is sparse; the campaign will have too little signal.

## Store and Landing Consistency

- Match the first three App Store screenshots to the campaign promises: adaptive workout, fast food logging, progress.
- Keep Premium and free-tier naming consistent.
- Ensure the App Store description does not imply every feature is free.
- Use the same teal/cyan/green visual language across ads, App Store screenshots, and onboarding.

## Video Plan

Google App campaigns perform better with video coverage, but videos must be hosted on YouTube. Until original video is approved and uploaded, allow Google to create automatic video assets from the submitted images and store listing.

Recommended first original video:

- Duration: 15 seconds
- Formats: 9:16 first, then 1:1 and 16:9
- Hook (0-3s): too many decisions, no clear next step
- Proof (3-11s): workout plan, food photo log, progress view
- Close (11-15s): `Turn goals into today with DoThis`
- No before/after body imagery and no unsupported result claims

Public YouTube upload and ad submission require separate approval.

## Launch Guardrails

- Do not launch until billing, daily budget, geography, conversion goal, and attribution are reviewed.
- Start with English-speaking United States traffic unless the App Store listing and support are localized elsewhere.
- Exclude minors from any audience settings that expose age controls.
- Review search terms and placements once reporting becomes available.
- Use a controlled test budget; scale only after activation and subscription economics are visible.
