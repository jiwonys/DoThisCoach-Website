Questions athletes ask before downloading.

Take a clear look at whether DoThis fits how you train.

Is DoThis only for one sport?

No. Current sport-specific acquisition states include Soccer, Basketball, Pickleball, Tennis, and Volleyball, with a general multi-sport option.

What happens when I have a match or sport day?

You confirm the day in Athlete Week. When generating training, DoThis can use that context and present Sports Prep, lighter, or full-workout choices where applicable.

Can I bring a workout I already use?

Yes. Workout import creates an editable preview so you can review the parsed exercise order and targets before saving.

Does the Preview start charging automatically?

No. The 7-day Premium Preview requires no payment and does not charge automatically.

Is DoThis medical advice?

No. DoThis provides educational fitness and nutrition support, not medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.
