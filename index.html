01 / SCHEDULE

Your week is input. Not something AI quietly rewrites.

Set Sport, Match Day, Gym, Cardio, Rest, or Not Sure for exact dates. Reuse last week or start fresh. Your confirmed week guides generation.

02 / DECISION

Before anything generates, you choose the dose.

On Match Day, choose Sports Prep, Lighter Workout, or Full Workout. DoThis coordinates training around the event without moving it.

  • Sports Prep: warm-up + cooldown
  • Lighter: shorter, lower-dose session
  • Full: normal session with match context
