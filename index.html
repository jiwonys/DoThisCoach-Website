A few things
before you
jump in.

Do I need to be an experienced athlete?

No. Tell DoThis your experience, goals, available equipment, and the sport you play. Review the plan and choose training that fits your situation.

Which sports does it support?

DoThis is for recreational athletes across sports, including tennis, basketball, volleyball, soccer, and pickleball. Your sport and schedule provide context for your strength training.

Will the free Preview charge me?

No. The one-time 7-day Premium Preview starts after your first personalized workout is ready. It requires no payment and does not turn into a subscription automatically.

What if my week changes?

Update your schedule and tell Coach what has changed. Review the guidance and confirm your training choices in the app.

Is Coach a real person?

Coach uses AI. It provides general fitness and nutrition support, not medical care or a replacement for a qualified professional. You make the final decision about your training.

Can I use DoThis on Android?

DoThis is currently available on iPhone. Download it from the App Store to get started.
