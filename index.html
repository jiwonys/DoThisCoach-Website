Do I need to be an experienced athlete?

No. Share your training experience, equipment, and goals. DoThis is for people who take their game seriously, whether they’re starting out or have trained for years.

Which sports can I explore?

Choose tennis, basketball, volleyball, pickleball, or soccer to see the relevant app preview. All sports opens the general DoThis experience.

What happens during the free Preview?

Your one-time 7-day Premium Preview begins after your first personalized workout is ready. No payment is required, and it does not charge automatically. You can choose Premium afterward.

What if my schedule or equipment changes?

Update your training settings and confirmed week, or tell Coach what changed. Review the suggested plan before training. You stay in control of what you do.

Can I log food and track progress too?

Yes. Log meals and macros, record the weights and reps you actually complete, and view your training progress in the same app.

Is DoThis available on Android?

DoThis is currently available for iPhone. The download links on this site lead to the Apple App Store.
