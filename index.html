The six essentials

Everything that moves the needle. Nothing that doesn't.

Daily Plan

One plan for the whole day

Strength, cardio or sport, meals, recovery, and goals — coordinated in a single Daily Plan. Training and playing today? It helps you pick the right load instead of guessing.

Workout

Sport-aware generation

Workouts built around your schedule, available equipment, recent sessions, and current limitations. Follow set by set, adjust on the fly, and let what you actually lifted shape what comes next.

Food + Progress

Log it. See it move.

Fast food logging, daily nutrition totals, weigh-ins, and goal tracking — with progress trends that show whether the work is working.

Coach

A coach that remembers

Ask about your sets, meals, weight trend, or this week's plan. Coach knows your context, remembers your preferences and limitations, and advises — you stay in control of every change.

AI Coach

Talk to someone who was there for every rep.

Your coach can see your workout history, food logs, weight trend, goals, and schedule — so advice is about your training, not training in general.

It remembers narrowly scoped preferences ("my left shoulder hates overhead press") and applies them to future plans. It suggests; you confirm.

Honest progression

A lighter lift before game night is a smart choice — not a step backward. Your bench doesn't forget.

Your weights anchor the plan

New plans start from the load you actually lifted — never a guess. Every progression tells you exactly why a weight went up, held, or eased off.

Game days don't cost you

A deliberately light session before your match stays a game-day choice. Your strength baseline holds steady.

Variety like a real coach

Back-to-back sessions rotate muscle groups the way a coach would — so you recover while you keep building.
