How it works

Your whole fitness life, one decision loop.

DoThis listens to the signals that affect an athlete's real week, then turns them into a concise day strategy and a single Daily Goals list.

Have a question? Contact support
  1. Set your sport, goal, and schedule. Tell DoThis when you play, lift, compete, work, and recover.
  2. Check in and execute. Log energy, soreness, training, food, and changes in your real day.
  3. Let the plan coordinate itself. Daily Goals update across training, nutrition, and recovery without becoming separate competing plans.
  4. Review meaningful changes. See what changed, why it changed, and approve or undo the decision.
