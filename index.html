More questions? Get in touch
Do I have to be a competitive athlete?

No. DoThis is built for people who lift and play a sport, including adult recreational players. Your goals, experience, equipment, and weekly schedule help shape your training.

Is this a human personal trainer?

DoThis is an AI-assisted training app. Coach uses your relevant training context to offer guidance, and you confirm changes yourself. It does not provide an in-person trainer or assess your exercise technique in person.

Can I ask for a specific kind of workout?

Yes. Share your training requests and preferences, use your available equipment, or import a workout you already follow. You can review and adjust workouts and manage what Coach remembers.

What happens after the free preview?

You keep access to saved workouts, local history, food and weight logging, goals, and core progress tools. Premium is required for new personalized AI workouts and plans, Coach, AI imports and edits, and cloud backup and sync. The preview never charges you automatically.

Can DoThis help with rehab or an injury?

You can tell DoThis about training limitations and recovery needs. It provides general fitness and planning support, not injury diagnosis, treatment, or rehabilitation care. Follow a qualified clinician’s guidance for an injury and stop any activity that causes pain.

Where can I download it?

DoThis is available on iPhone. Get it on the App Store and build your first personalized workout to start your preview.
