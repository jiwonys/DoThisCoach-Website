A few quick answers.

Do I have to be a serious athlete?

No. DoThis is for people who want their gym training to support the sport they enjoy. Share your experience, equipment, and goals to give your workouts context.

Which sports can I train for?

DoThis supports recreational athletes across sports, including tennis, volleyball, basketball, soccer, and pickleball. Your sport and weekly schedule help shape your strength training.

Is Coach a person?

Coach uses AI and provides general fitness support. Review its guidance and make your own training decisions. It is not medical care or a replacement for a qualified professional.
