How it works

Start with Today. Build the rest from there.

Each section has one job, so planning, training, food, coaching, goals, and progress stay easy to understand.

Have a question? Contact support
  1. Set your goal and routine. Add your goal, weekly workout or sport schedule, experience, equipment, and coaching preferences.
  2. Open Today first. Check energy, add an optional weigh-in, and use your Daily Goals as a flexible checklist.
  3. Train and fuel honestly. Generate or import workouts, log what you complete, and record food with the fastest accurate method available.
  4. Ask Coach and review progress. Explain what changed, ask a specific question, and use trends—not one perfect day—to decide what comes next.
