How it works

Start with Today. Build the rest from there.

Each section has one job, so planning, training, food, coaching, goals, and progress stay easy to understand.

Have a question? Contact support
  1. Tell it your week. Your sport and match days, your training days, experience, equipment, and coaching style. Two minutes, once.
  2. Open Today first. One list for the day: the workout, protein and calorie targets, and recovery—coordinated around what you’re playing this week.
  3. Train and fuel honestly. Log the weights you actually used and the food you actually ate. Tomorrow’s plan adapts to tonight, including the game.
  4. Ask Coach and review progress. Explain what changed, ask a specific question, and use trends—not one perfect day—to decide what comes next.

Progression you can audit

Built on weights you actually lifted.

No guessed one-rep maxes. DoThis anchors to the loads you logged and edited yourself, holds steady until you’ve put in clean sessions, then moves you up—and tells you why in plain language.

Is this just a chatbot?

No. The chat is the smallest part. DoThis is a planning engine: it builds your workouts, coordinates nutrition and recovery, and adapts progression from your logged training. The AI’s job is memory and adaptation—remembering your constraints and bending the plan around your schedule—not conversation for its own sake.

Do I need a credit card for the free week?

No. No payment method is collected for the Premium Preview, which means it cannot charge you when it ends. If you want Premium after 7 days, you subscribe through the App Store yourself.

Which sports does it support?

60+ sports—volleyball, basketball, soccer, tennis, climbing, martial arts, and more. You pick your primary sport during setup, schedule sessions on any day, and plans coordinate around them.

What stays free if I don’t subscribe?

Your saved workouts, workout history, food and weight logging, goals, and core progress tools. That data lives on your device and stays yours. Premium adds new AI plans, workout generation, Coach chat with memory, and private cloud backup and sync.
