# DoThis creative direction

## Read first
Read applicable AGENTS.md, the current approved design brief, PRODUCT.md, DESIGN.md, and the asset/destination manifests. Locate the actual website workspace. Preserve existing brand assets and unrelated work. When present, use Impeccable as the primary general design skill; this skill supplies DoThis-specific constraints.

## Outcome
Make an athlete recognize the sport, understand DoThis, see authentic product evidence, and find the appropriate App Store action. Award-caliber is a craft aspiration, not a factual award or conversion guarantee.

## Reference work
Inspect https://subscrr.app/ and https://dothiscoach.com/ in the browser on desktop and mobile. Capture useful states. Record observations separately from inferences. Extract hierarchy, product scale, typography, pacing, and purposeful interactions. Do not copy the reference's branding, assets, code, text, or privacy promises. Do not claim unperformed visual inspection.

## Art direction
Use a premium editorial sports-brand approach with real app screens. Explore at most two distinct compositions before full implementation. Sample the approved brand assets for color, verify font licensing, and use a restrained type system. A light editorial option and a darker athletic option are useful exploration directions, not mandatory final colors.

Proposed starting proposition: “Train for your game. Not just the gym.” Verify all supporting product claims. Speak to recreational soccer, basketball, pickleball, tennis, and volleyball athletes without implying only one sport is supported.

No logo-orbit illustration, random dots, particle field, decorative node network, generic gradient hero, fake dashboard, or placeholder SVG/canvas/CSS artwork in place of professional imagery. Existing SVG logos, useful icons, separators, and meaningful charts are allowed. Do not force an arbitrary graphic motif across every section.

## Product-led narrative
Lead with the sport-relevant benefit and show a current screen. Use a coherent sport selector tied to actual imagery, screenshot content, and correct acquisition destination. Follow with one understandable training situation, focused product benefit chapters, honest trust, verified preview/Premium terms, and a clear final CTA. Preserve useful guides and legal/support navigation.

Avoid internal engineering vocabulary as headline copy, repetitive phone/card layouts, tiny illegible UI, fake urgency, invented proof, or guaranteed medical/athletic outcomes. Do not assume a repository feature has shipped.

## Approval and iteration
Before building the whole page, produce two working hero-plus-selector prototypes and browser screenshots at mobile/desktop sizes. Recommend one and stop for approval. Lock the decision in DESIGN.md. Refinement should improve the approved concept, not reopen the whole art direction.

Use CSS and the existing motion stack first. Add no library solely for prestige. Keep native scrolling, immediate content visibility, reduced-motion support, and touch/keyboard usability.

## Completion evidence
Report actual prototype/implementation files, screenshots, approved design decisions, source assets, and unresolved product claims. Never present an image-generated page mockup as browser-rendered implementation.
